# ⚡ OmniLoader - 100% Ad-Free Universal Video Downloader

A clean, modern, and high-speed personal video downloader that works with zero ads, zero redirects, and zero limits.

## ✨ Supported Platforms
- **YouTube**: 4K, 1080p Full HD, 720p, 480p, MP3 Audio (with FFmpeg auto-merging)
- **TikTok**: Without Watermark HD downloads
- **Instagram**: Reels, Stories, Posts
- **Facebook**: Public Videos & Reels
- **Twitter / X**: Video clips & GIFs
- **Reddit, Pinterest, Vimeo, Twitch, and 1000+ more platforms!**

---

## 🚀 How to Run Anytime

1. Simply double-click **`start.bat`** inside this folder.
2. It will automatically start the server and open your browser at:
   👉 **http://localhost:3000**
3. Paste any video link, choose your preferred quality (or MP3), and click **Download Now**!

---

## 📁 Where Are Videos Saved?
All downloaded videos are automatically saved in the local **`downloads/`** folder inside this directory, and also delivered directly to your browser's default Downloads folder.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack Under the Hood
- **Frontend**: Clean glassmorphism UI with real-time download speed, ETA & progress tracking
- **Backend**: Node.js & Express
- **Download Engine**: `yt-dlp` (Latest release)
- **Media Processing**: `FFmpeg` (For merging high-res video + audio streams & MP3 conversion)
